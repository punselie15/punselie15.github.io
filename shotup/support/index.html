Party Up (iPhone) and Shot Up (Android)

Party Up and Shot Up are the same pass-and-play party card game, made by Punselie. Party Up is the name on the App Store and Shot Up is the name on Google Play.

Contact us

Found a bug, have a question about a purchase, or want to suggest a card? Open a ticket at our support page on GitHub (free GitHub account needed). We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

What is a penalty?
Your group decides before you start: 5 push-ups, a silly dance, a forfeit or an extra truth question. The game only tells you who takes how many.

How do I unlock a game mode?
Tap a locked mode on the menu. You can watch ads to unlock it, buy it once, or buy "All Modes + No Ads" to unlock everything and remove all ads.

I bought something and it's gone.
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the main menu while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play refunds.

How do I turn off personalised ads?
On iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android: Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Buying "All Modes + No Ads" removes ads entirely.

Who is the game for?
Adults (18+). Only play along with what you're comfortable with, and skip any card you don't like.
