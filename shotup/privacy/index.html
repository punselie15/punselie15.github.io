Privacy Policy

Shot Up (Android) and Party Up (iPhone) · Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the party game apps Shot Up (Google Play, package com.Punselie.ShotUp) and Party Up (App Store, bundle com.Punselie.PartyUp), together "the app", handle, and why. Both apps are made by the same independent developer, Punselie ("we", "us"). They are the same game under a different name on each store.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Player names you type in and your settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The app is meant for adults (18+).

Information stored only on your device

To run the game, the app saves a few things locally on your phone: the player names and colours you enter for a game, your settings, whether you've seen the introduction screen, and which game modes you've unlocked. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you haven't bought the "no ads" option, the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version,
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic information about ad loading.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. You can read how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads; iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). Buying "no ads" stops the app from loading ads entirely.

In-app purchases

Game modes and the "all modes + no ads" bundle are sold through Google Play Billing or Apple's App Store. Your payment is processed entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. The app only receives a confirmation (receipt) that a purchase was made, so it can unlock what you paid for and restore it with "Restore purchases". We do not receive your name, email address or payment card details.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers that collect data from the app, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Age requirement

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected about them, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Data retention and deletion

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or its data. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies. Because we never receive personal data from the app, there is no account or server-side data for us to delete; if you have a question or request, contact us below and we will help you exercise it with Google where needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can withdraw ad consent by resetting your advertising ID, changing the settings described above, or reinstalling the app to see the consent form again. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is exchanged with Google's ad services over encrypted connections. Information stored on your device is protected by your phone's own security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your privacy, email the developer at the contact address shown on the app's page in Google Play or the App Store.
